WHO WE ARE

Innovation That Goes Beyond the Classroom

ALTERVENTION is an innovation-driven startup based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, focused on transforming student academic projects into affordable, practical products for real-world use. Our core belief is that student ideas should not end with submission — they should evolve into products that solve real problems. We specialize in affordable IoT-based home automation including water level indicators, mobile-controlled appliances, smart energy meters, presence-based automation, and sound-triggered lighting systems.
